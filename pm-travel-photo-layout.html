PM Travel Agency · Photography brief

The shot list

Every hatched area on this page is one photograph. The brief for that exact shot is written inside it. Shoot from the top down: Group A is worth more than Groups B and C combined, because nobody else can take it.

What this is for

This is a business-to-business website. The audience is travel professionals deciding whether to trust us with their own clients — not holidaymakers choosing a destination. They already know Morocco is beautiful; they sell it. What they do not know is whether we can move forty people from Marrakech airport to a hotel without a problem.

So the brief is unusual: the unglamorous operational photographs matter more than the scenery. One picture of our own minibus at a hotel entrance, with the driver beside it, does more for this site than ten sunset dunes.

Style

  • Editorial, not advertising. Travel journalism, not brochure.
  • Natural light. Real situations, real people working.
  • Restrained colour. Architecture and depth over close-up detail.
  • People small in frame and incidental, never posed at camera — except the team portraits, where they look straight down the lens.
  • Leave empty space in wide shots for headline text to sit.

Please avoid

  • Heavy filters, oversaturation, unrealistic grading.
  • Golden-hour overuse. Fisheye. Heavy vignettes.
  • Posed smiling at camera in operational shots.
  • Clichés: lantern walls, spice pyramids, posed camel silhouettes, tagine close-ups, snake charmers.
  • Jemaa el-Fna at sunset. Every competitor has it.

Colour

The site palette is stone, deep teal and burnt oxide. Warm neutral tones and muted colour sit well against it. Very blue or very green images will fight the design.

Group A · shoot first

Operational and team

These cannot be bought at any price, which is exactly why they are worth the most. If the budget covers only one half day, shoot A1, A2, A3 and A5 — office, team, a vehicle, and an airport meet.

A1

Office, Gueliz — exterior and interior

The real space, real people working, natural light. Not staged, not tidied into a showroom. This is the single most important trust image on the site.

Use: About page lead · Ratio: 16:9 and a 3:2 alternate
A3

Vehicle at a hotel entrance

Our actual vehicle, clean, plate visible, driver standing beside it. Not moving, not artful — the buyer wants to see that it exists and that it looks presentable outside their client's hotel.

Agafay is not sand

Agafay is grey-brown stone desert. Most images labelled "Agafay" online are actually Merzouga or Erg Chebbi. If we publish a dune photograph labelled Agafay, any buyer who knows Morocco spots it immediately — and the entire verification story this site is built on dies with it. Check every desert frame against what Agafay actually looks like.

Group C

Original only, and only when there is a real event. A staged banquet photograph fools nobody in this audience.

Never photograph a real client event and publish it without written permission from that client. No exceptions.

Rights — please read

The contract must assign PM Travel unlimited commercial usage rights for website, print, social media and advertising, with no time limit. A photographer retaining rights while we build a brand on their images is a problem we do not want later. This needs to be in writing before the shoot, not after.

People in frame

A signed model release is needed from anyone recognisable — staff, guides, drivers, clients. A simple one-page form. No release, no publication, so please track who appears in which frame as you shoot.
