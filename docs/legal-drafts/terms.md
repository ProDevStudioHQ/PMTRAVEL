## Who this site is for

This is a business-to-business site. It is intended for travel trade
professionals — tour operators, travel agencies and destination specialists. It
does not sell travel to the public and does not take consumer bookings.

## Quotations and service status

Nothing on this website is an offer. A quotation is an offer only when we issue
it, and every line in it carries an explicit status: requested, pending, on
option, held, confirmed, cancelled, expired or estimated.

**Only a service marked confirmed is contracted.** A service on option or held
may be released.

## Operational information on this site

Where we publish an operational figure — a distance, a drive time, a capacity —
we publish alongside it how it was measured, when, and by whom. Figures are
measured by us and are accurate as at the verification date shown. Conditions
change; a figure is a record of a measurement, not a guarantee of a future
journey.

Where we have not measured something, the site says so rather than estimating.
