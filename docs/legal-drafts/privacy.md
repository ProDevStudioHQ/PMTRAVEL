## What we collect

We collect personal data in one place only: the quote request form, and any
file attached to it.

**Required:** company, country, contact name, role, email address,
destinations, travel dates, number of travellers, programme type, and the brief
you write.

**Optional:** rooming and hotel detail, transport, guide language, meals,
activities, budget range, quote deadline, accessibility needs, special
requirements, previous Morocco experience, and how you found us.

**Automatically:** a salted, one-way hash of your IP address, used to
rate-limit submissions, and your browser's user agent string. We do not store
your IP address itself.

## What we do not collect

We use no analytics of any kind. We set no cookies. We embed no third-party
content. We do not track you across sites, and the site's content security
policy prevents any third-party script from loading at all.
